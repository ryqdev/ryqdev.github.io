*A sample article.*

There is something inviting about a personal website. Its shape can follow the
interests of one person: a collection of notes, a reading list, an unfinished
experiment, or a photograph with a few lines beneath it.

## Make room for the specific

A small site does not have to cover a whole subject. One carefully described
observation is enough for a page. Over time, those pages begin to form their own
map of interests.

## Follow a link

A link is a small invitation to leave one page and discover another. A list of
favorite sites can be as personal as a bookshelf, with unexpected neighbors and
room for something new.

The site can grow slowly. It can remain small. Both are good reasons to keep
writing.
