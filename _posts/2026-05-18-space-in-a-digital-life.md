*A sample article.*

One fewer icon on the desktop or an empty half hour in the calendar might not
seem like much of a change. But the point of leaving space is to make room for
ideas that have not arrived yet.

## Start with something small

There is no need to organize every file or replace your entire note-taking
system. Write today's tasks on one page and close the windows you do not need
right now. Making the work in front of you clearer is enough.

## Allow time without an output

Take a walk, leaf through a book, or simply sit for a while. Not every moment
needs to produce a result. Sometimes it is worth setting aside the urge to
document everything and giving the experience itself your attention.

The number of tools can keep growing. Life does not have to become more crowded
along with them.
