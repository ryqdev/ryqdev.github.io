*A sample article.*

A useful workspace does not have to put everything within sight. Sometimes the
best improvement is to close a panel, clear a surface, or leave one window open.

## Start with one thing

Choose the task before choosing the tool. A page of notes and a single editor
window can be enough to begin. Everything else can wait until it is needed.

## Leave a place to return to

Before stopping, write one sentence about the next step. It is a small gesture
that makes the following session easier to enter.

The aim is not a perfect desk. It is a workspace that asks for a little less
attention than the work itself.
